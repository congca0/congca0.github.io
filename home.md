I am a <strong style="color: #8B0000;">Causal AI researcher</strong> working at the intersection of <strong style="color: #8B0000;">causal inference, machine learning, and population health</strong>. I develop quantitative methods for studying complex biomedical and real-world health data to better understand variation in health and treatment outcomes. My work addresses fundamental questions in <strong style="color: #8B0000;">population health and precision medicine</strong>, with applications to treatment effects and real-world evidence.

I received my PhD in Economics, with a focus on machine learning, from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023. I have previously held research and teaching positions at Yale University, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, and Caltech. I am currently a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University.

I also serve as a Nordic Co-Editor of the *Scandinavian Journal of Public Health* and an Associate Editor of *Biometrics*.

<strong style="color: #8B0000;">Research Interests:</strong> Causal AI · Machine Learning · Population Health · Precision Medicine · Real-World Evidence
