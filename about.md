## About

I am a Postdoctoral Scholar in the Department of Surgery at Stanford University and a Research Statistician with the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

My work combines causal inference, AI, machine learning, and biomedical data science. I am particularly interested in developing methods that can help us better understand complex biomedical data and make better treatment decisions.

I received my PhD in Economics, with a focus on machine learning, from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023. Before joining Stanford, I worked at Yale and Caltech.
