## About

I am a Causal AI scientist developing methods for biomedical and clinical research. I am a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University. I received my PhD in Economics, with a focus on machine learning, from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023. I previously held research and teaching appointments at Yale University/the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, and Caltech.

My research lies at the intersection of causal inference, statistical machine learning, and artificial intelligence. I develop causal AI methods to analyze complex biomedical data, uncover mechanisms underlying health outcomes and treatment effects, and inform precision treatment decisions.

**Associate Editor:** *Biometrics* · *Scandinavian Journal of Public Health*
