## Research

My research combines **causal inference, statistical machine learning, and multimodal biomedical data** to understand why patients respond differently to the same treatment.

<div class="research-grid">

<div class="research-card">

### 01 · Treatment Effect Heterogeneity

<p class="research-question">Who benefits?</p>

I develop causal inference and machine learning methods to identify heterogeneous treatment effects and characterize which patients are most likely to benefit from a given intervention.

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### 02 · Mechanism Discovery

<p class="research-question">Why does treatment work?</p>

I develop representation learning and causal mediation methods to identify biological and behavioral pathways underlying heterogeneous treatment effects.

</div>

<div class="research-card">

### 03 · Clinical Decision-Making

<p class="research-question">How can we use this knowledge?</p>

I develop mechanism-informed approaches for treatment selection, transportability, and precision clinical decision-making.

</div>

</div>

## Selected Work

### Mechanism-Informed Causal AI for Precision Treatment Decisions

I develop causal AI methods that integrate treatment-effect heterogeneity with mechanistic information to support individualized treatment decisions in metabolic disease.

**Current project · Stanford University**

---

### Multimodal Representation Learning for Mechanism Discovery

I develop multimodal representation learning methods to uncover latent mediation structures across socioeconomic, psychosocial, clinical, laboratory, behavioral, and genomic information.

**Cao & Ma · 2026**

[View paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.04016)

---

### Causal Inference under Interference with Learned Exposure Mappings

I study causal inference when individuals' outcomes may depend on the treatments received by others, with a focus on learning exposure mappings from complex data.

**Current research**
