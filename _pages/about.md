# Cong Cao, PhD

Research Fellow  
Department of Surgery  
Stanford University

I develop mechanism-informed causal AI methods to improve health.

My research combines causal inference, statistical machine learning, and multimodal biomedical data. I study why patients respond differently to the same treatment and develop methods for treatment effect heterogeneity, disease mechanism discovery, and mechanism-informed clinical decision-making.

Before joining Stanford, I was a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at Yale School of Public Health and a Research Statistician at the CSP Coordinating Center, VA Connecticut Healthcare System.

I was previously a Postdoctoral Teaching Fellow at the California Institute of Technology (2023–2025). I received my PhD in Economics, with a specialization in Machine Learning, from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023.

I am currently on the 2026–2027 academic job market and am seeking tenure-track faculty positions.

## Research Interests

Causal AI · Causal Inference · Statistical Machine Learning · Treatment Effect Heterogeneity · Biomedical Data Science
