I am a <strong style="color: #8B0000;">Causal AI researcher</strong> working at the intersection of <strong style="color: #8B0000;">causal inference, machine learning, and AI</strong>. I develop mechanism-informed causal AI methods to study complex biomedical and real-world data and better understand variation in health outcomes.  

I received my PhD in Economics, with a focus on machine learning, from the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023. I have previously held research and teaching positions at Yale University, the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, and Caltech. I am currently a Postdoctoral Scholar at Stanford University. I also serve as a Nordic Co-Editor of the *Scandinavian Journal of Public Health* and an Associate Editor of *Biometrics*.

<strong style="color: #8B0000;">Research Interests:</strong> Mechanism-Informed Causal AI · Population Health · Precision Medicine · Real-World Evidence
